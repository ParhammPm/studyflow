# Privacy Policy — StudyFlow

StudyFlow is a local, single-user study planner. It runs on your own computer.

**Your data stays yours.** Your study programme, test results, exam data and
settings are stored only on your own computer (and, if you enable it, in your
own private Backblaze B2 bucket for backup). Nothing is sent to us or to any
third party. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

**Google Calendar.** If you connect your Google account, the app accesses
your Google Calendar only to synchronise your study programme: it reads your
calendar events, and it creates, updates or deletes events for boxes you add
or edit in the app. Access happens only after you sign in with your own
Google account (OAuth 2.0); the access and refresh tokens are stored only on
your own computer. You can disconnect at any time from the app's Backup tab,
which revokes the access.

**Google Gemini.** If you enter a Gemini API key, it is stored only on your
own computer and used only to call Google's Gemini API on your behalf.

**Questions.** Open an issue at https://github.com/ParhammPm/studyflow
